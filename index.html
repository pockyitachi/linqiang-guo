02 — About

Research at the intersection of AI and software systems.

My work focuses on intelligent mobile software systems — designing agents that combine large language models, vision-language pipelines, and finite-state reasoning to automate and test complex mobile applications, and to stay state-aware, robust, and recoverable when things go wrong. I'm also a Graduate Student Researcher at Mila — Quebec Artificial Intelligence Institute, working on RL-based post-training (GRPO, DPO) for multimodal GUI agents.

Before my PhD I built and maintained microservices for customer personalization and recommendation at Walmart Global Tech, integrating GenAI into search and tuning Milvus on Kubernetes. I hold an MS in ECE (AI track) from the University of Miami and a BS in Electrical Engineering from Missouri.

03 — Publications

Selected Papers

  • ECCV 2026 WS

    Learning to Adapt: Few-Shot Generalization of Mobile Agents to Unseen Applications

    Siobhan Reid, Li Gu, Zhixiang Chi, Linqiang Guo, Zihuan Jiang, Ziqiang Wang, Yuanhao Yu, Yang Wang

    Best Paper Award · ECCV 2026 Workshop on Multimodal Digital Agents (MDA) · Spotlight Talk + Poster

  • ECCV 2026 WS

    Q-Reflector: Learning GUI Action Reflection with Generated Conditions

    Linqiang Guo, Wei Liu, Li Gu, Yang Wang, Tse-Hsun (Peter) Chen

    ECCV 2026 Workshop on Multimodal Digital Agents (MDA) · Poster + Lightning Talk · arXiv · Demo · Code

    A standalone Qwen3-VL-8B reflector that first generates an action’s pre- and post-conditions, then checks the before/after screenshots to judge whether the action succeeded and actually moved the task forward. It reaches 82.16% accuracy on 1,082 verified AndroidWorld transitions and lifts MobileWorld task success by up to 17 points when plugged into GUI agents.

  • ECCV 2026 WS

    CoAdapt-GUI: Joint Workflow Context and Policy Adaptation for Unseen GUI Applications

    Linqiang Guo, Li Gu, Zihuan Jiang, Zhixiang Chi, Siobhan Reid, Ziqiang Wang, Yuanhao Yu, Wei Liu, Yang Wang, Tse-Hsun (Peter) Chen

    ECCV 2026 Workshop on Multimodal Digital Agents (MDA) · Poster + Lightning Talk · arXiv

    A test-time adaptation framework that lets a mobile GUI agent adapt to an app it has never seen, jointly refining a transferable workflow context and a LoRA policy from its own rollouts and rewards. It reaches 45.0% on AndroidWorld-Generalization (vs. 37.5% for policy-only adaptation) and raises AndroidWorld Plus from 38.6% to 52.9%.

  • ECCV 2026 WS

    Beyond the GUI Paradigm: Do Mobile Agents Need the Phone Screen?

    Li Gu, Zihuan Jiang, Linqiang Guo, Zhixiang Chi, Ziqiang Wang, Huan Liu, Yuanhao Yu, Tse-Hsun (Peter) Chen, Yang Wang

    ECCV 2026 Workshop on Multimodal Digital Agents (MDA) · Poster · arXiv

    Asks whether mobile agents really need the screen by letting off-the-shelf coding agents operate Android through the command line instead of the GUI. Claude Code reaches 71.8% on AndroidWorld and 51.9% on MobileWorld, beating every reproducible GUI baseline, and every CLI agent wins on the new CLI-Advantage suite with far fewer steps.

  • arXiv 2026

    AgentPersonaBench: Benchmarking Persona-Driven User Simulation

    Jintao Huang, Yifan Wang, Hongyu Shen, … , Linqiang Guo, … , Xiaomin Li, Yuexing Hao (46 authors)

    Preprint · 2,460-task benchmark across survey, chat, web & app surfaces · arXiv · Code

    A 2,460-task benchmark that tests whether a persona assigned to an agent actually steers what it does, judged only from observable actions across survey, chat, web and desktop-app environments. Leading models reach up to 84.7% full-pass adherence, yet adherence drops across interaction surfaces and under multi-attribute personas.

  • AAAI 2026

    Agent-SAMA: State-Aware Mobile Assistant

    Linqiang Guo, Wei Liu, Yi Wen Heng, Yang Wang, Tse-Hsun (Peter) Chen

    AAAI 2026 · arXiv · Code

    A state-aware multi-agent framework that models app execution as a finite state machine — screens as states, actions as transitions — which four specialized agents build in real time to plan, verify each step and recover from errors. It reaches 84.0% success on Mobile-Eval-E, 80.0% on SPA-Bench and 63.7% on AndroidWorld, improving task success by up to 12% and recovery by 13.8% over prior methods.

  • ICSE 2025

    GUIWatcher: Automatically Detecting GUI Lags by Analyzing Mobile Application Screencasts

    Wei Liu, Feng Lin, Linqiang Guo, Tse-Hsun (Peter) Chen, Ahmed E. Hassan

    ICSE 2025 · arXiv

    A framework that detects GUI lags in screencasts recorded during mobile app testing, using computer vision to find janky, long-loading and frozen frames and to prioritize the most severe ones. It reaches 0.91 precision and 0.96 recall on real-world app tests and runs continuously in a production testing pipeline.

  • COLM 2025 WS

    MAPLE: A Mobile Assistant with Persistent Finite State Machines for Recovery Reasoning

    Linqiang Guo, Wei Liu, Yi Wen Heng, Yang Wang, Tse-Hsun (Peter) Chen

    The workshop version of the Agent-SAMA idea: a mobile assistant that keeps a persistent finite-state-machine memory of the app’s navigation flow, with screens as states and actions as transitions, so it can check its progress and reason its way back after errors. The approach was later extended into Agent-SAMA (AAAI 2026).

  • CMIG

    Registration on DCE-MRI Images via Multi-Domain Image-to-Image Translation

    Naxin Cai, Houjin Chen, Yanfeng Li, Yahui Peng, Linqiang Guo

    Computerized Medical Imaging and Graphics (IF 7.422)

  • Under Review

    Screencast-Based Analysis of User-Perceived GUI Responsiveness

    Wei Liu, Linqiang Guo, Yi Wen Heng, Chenglin Li, Tse-Hsun (Peter) Chen, Ahmed E. Hassan

    Under review · arXiv

    Presents MobileGUIPerf, a lightweight black-box technique that measures GUI responsiveness straight from testing screencasts, computing response time and finish time for every user interaction. On 2,458 annotated interactions from 64 Android apps it detects interactions with 0.96 precision and 0.93 recall, and it analyzes thousands of screencasts daily in an industrial pipeline.

  • Under Review

    PopSweeper: Automatically Detecting and Resolving App-Blocking Pop-Ups to Assist Automated Mobile GUI Testing

    Linqiang Guo, Wei Liu, Yi Wen Heng, Yang Wang, Tse-Hsun (Peter) Chen

    A lightweight, screenshot-based tool that detects app-blocking pop-ups during automated GUI testing and locates the control to close them — such pop-ups appeared in 42.5% of the recent top apps studied. Paired with DroidBot, it cut time lost to pop-ups by 59.27% and pop-up-caused redirects by 80%.
